Formed in 1936, the Hertford & District Table tennis league celebrates 90 years of Table Tennis this year and is
quite probably the 13th oldest TT league in UK, this season covering an area from Bishops Stortford to Southbury Road, Enfield.
The league is affiliated to Table Tennis England and the Hertfordshire County Table Tennis Association.

For the 2026/27 season there are 10 clubs in the league, providing 26 teams spread over the 3 divisions,
competing in regular league matches as well as cup and special tournament fixtures.
This season we've gained a 2nd Ellenborough team and a 5th Water Lane team.

New players, teams and clubs of all standards are always welcome - contact any of our committee for details:
