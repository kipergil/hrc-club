The league season finally finished with Cheshunt IV meeting HRC II in the last match in Division 1.   Cheshunt IV won 8-2, finishing just one point behind champions Cheshunt III who they recently pipped 5-4 in the Divisional Final.  Alex Morealle finished top of the division averages, losing one match all season but he came close to losing number two.  In probably the longest league match in the five set format, he finally overcame Mike Roberts 12-14, 9-11, 16-14, 15-13,16-14 in a match totalling 134 points.
